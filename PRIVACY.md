# Privacy as Method — STILL HERE

The public site tells the history without exposing living relatives. No names, dates, photographs, direct chains of relationship, contact details, private certificates, tribal enrollment information, raw GEDCOMs, or private correspondence involving living people are published by default.

The separate private family-history HTML contains names of living relatives and is for the steward's access-controlled storage only. **Do not upload it to GitHub, including a private folder within a public repository**. A file in a public repo can remain accessible in commit history even after deletion.

Named deceased historical persons may appear in the public exhibit when essential to evaluating a source. Every identity or relationship claim must be marked as family-reported, document-supported, hypothesis, or unknown. Sovereign tribal nations determine their own citizenship; no claim is made from a surname or an oral-history association alone.

Permission-cleared documents may be added only after reviewing rights, identifying details, and historical context. Published records and online indexes do not automatically authorize republication of their images.
