# Source register and citation guide

**R01 — _A History of Crystal Township_**  
https://www.migenweb.org/oceana/history/CrystalTwpHistory.pdf  
Research lead: mentions an Anna Pawneshing, John Buckobanow, and Seaman residents, and reports a historical kinship claim involving Chief Cobmoosa. **A same-name mention is not a proven match to the family's ancestor.** Verify printed page, wording, and original supporting records.

**R02 — St. Joseph cemetery photo index (Oceana County)**  
https://migenweb.org/archive_html/tsphoto/oceana/stjosephscath.htm  
Research lead: same-name Pawneshing entries. Inspect original gravestones, dates, and independent records before attributing identities.

**R03 — _History of Ionia County_ (secondary history)**  
https://ldsgenealogy.com/MI/books/History-of-Ionia-County-Michigan-her-people-industries-and-institutions-Volume-I-part-42.htm  
Research lead: describes Cobmoosa's relocation. Does not establish family descent.

**R04 — Bureau of Indian Affairs historical narrative**  
https://www.bia.gov/sites/default/files/media_document/146_narr_2000.pdf  
Historical context only; assess document provenance and limitations before use.

**R05 — National Archives Native American research guide**  
https://www.archives.gov/research/native-americans/rolls  
Research methodology; not a source of proof for a specific person.

**R06 — BIA tracing ancestry guidance**  
https://www.bia.gov/guide/tracing-american-indian-and-alaska-native-aian-ancestry  
Distinguishes genealogical research from tribal citizenship.

**F01 — Family account, privately retained**  
Maternal and paternal family names and relationships supplied by the research steward. The private dossier retains fuller context. Family attestation is not equivalent to a primary certificate.

## Claim-level standard
For each future claim record the exact assertion, source author/title, publication or archive, image/page, date accessed, whether it is original or derivative, alternative explanations, privacy review, and status. Keep unconfirmed identities separate.
