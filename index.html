Chapter 03 / The investigation

Case files: what the archive actually says

Case 01 — Two women named Anna?

A History of Crystal Township refers to Anna Pawneshing, calls John Buckobanow her grandfather and describes her as a great-niece of Chief Cobmoosa. Family memory also includes an Anna Pawneshing, possibly with the surname Seaman. These may or may not be the same person. Identity must be tested with dates, marriage documentation, parents, burial and household records.

Source: Crystal Township history (PDF) · Identity unproven

Case 02 — The Seaman question

The same township account names Charles J. Seaman and Ralph Seaman in its regional history. A matching surname does not make either man a relative. Test Anna's birth surname against marriage certificates, probate, land deeds, and original census households.

Source: Crystal Township history · Hypothesis

Case 03 — Cobmoosa, Buckobanow and historical kinship

The reported great-niece relationship is historically interesting but cannot yet be attached to Bart Salazar's lineage. A documentary bridge must connect the family-reported Anna to the historically described Anna, then document the claimed intervening generations. Consult community-led history and original records; distinguish Odawa and Potawatomi histories rather than combining them.

Secondary Ionia County history · BIA historical report

Case 04 — Salazar across borders

Family tradition associates Bartolo Felix Salazar with Mexico. Without a confirmed town, dates, and records, a precise migration narrative would be fiction. Next steps are family-held certificates, civil and parish records, and corroborated census or immigration documents.

Case 05 — Jones, Morgan and northern Michigan

Family history places Lois Jones Morgan in northern Michigan and reports Potawatomi ancestry. Investigate her documented family history separately from tribal citizenship questions, which are determined by sovereign tribal nations.

Chapter 05 / What comes next

Documents still needed

Marriage and death records for Anna and Joseph Pawneshing; records linking them to Alma; original proof of Anna's birth surname; land and probate records for Buckobanow and Seaman families; confirmed Mexican civil or parish records for Bartolo Felix Salazar; original northern Michigan documents for Lois Jones Morgan. These are not footnotes to hide: they are the next chapters.

Private research edition: a separate local HTML dossier preserves the fuller family-reported relationships, without exposing living family members through GitHub Pages.

Privacy is part of the method.

Living relatives' identifying information, unredacted family records, tribal enrollment details, private correspondence, and raw genealogical datasets are excluded from this public edition. Historical documents are cited rather than reproduced without review.
