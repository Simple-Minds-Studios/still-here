Anna Pawneshing and the Crystal Township historical account

A local history mentions an Anna Pawneshing and describes a relationship to Chief Cobmoosa. Whether that person is the Anna in the family's oral history requires independent verification through dates, marriage, burial, and parent-child records.

The Seaman name and regional land history

Investigate local census, deed, probate, and cemetery records to test whether the family Seaman line intersects with Seaman households recorded in Oceana County. Similar surnames alone do not establish kinship.

How we evaluate evidence

01 · Documented

A source directly supports the stated claim; its limitations are recorded.

02 · Family-attested

A named family informant provides an account, stored privately with consent.

03 · Working hypothesis

A promising connection requiring corroboration.

04 · Unknown

An open question, not a blank to be filled with invention.

Privacy is part of the method.

Living relatives' identifying information, unredacted family records, tribal enrollment details, private correspondence, and raw genealogical datasets are excluded from this public edition. Historical documents are cited rather than reproduced without review.
