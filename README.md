# STILL HERE — A Tree of Memory

**Public edition 3.0 · October 2026**  
**Research steward: Bart Salazar** · Simple Minds Studios

Live: https://simple-minds-studios.github.io/still-here/

## What this is
A substantive, source-conscious family-history exhibition. Its narrative covers the maternal Pawneshing–Seaman–Vargas and paternal Salazar–Jones–Morgan investigations, family-reported generations, regional historical context, a timeline, five research case files, an evidence register, and a source library.

## What is established
Family testimony identifies Anna and Joseph Pawneshing, Alma Pawneshing Vargas and Joseph Vargas, Bartolo Felix Salazar and Lois Jones Morgan. Their family relationships have **not yet been independently documented**. A Crystal Township history refers to an Anna Pawneshing and to a reported Cobmoosa relationship, but the historical Anna has **not** been established as the family's Anna. Neither Indigenous citizenship nor tribal affiliation is inferred.

## Privacy
The public repository excludes names and relationships of living family members, full private genealogical records, identifying dates, enrollment records, GEDCOMs, and unredacted documents. The full private HTML is distributed separately and **must never be committed to this public repository**. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
